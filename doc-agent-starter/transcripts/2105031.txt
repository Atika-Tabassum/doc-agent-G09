## Stage 4 (Index) — detailed design notes

The table row above is the required per-facet summary;

reasoning, trade-offs, and verification evidence behin

**Chunking:r-sentence or
per-paragraph.** `chunk.split()` groups

`chunk_tokens=256` tokens across it, stepping forward

line-level input has no reliable sentence-boundary sig

scanned page's physical layout, not sentence structure

chunk can end mid-sentence; overlap (below) is what ke

**Overlap: 32 tokens, preserving continuity across the

with the next window's first 32 — verified directly by
`test_split_merges_lines_within_a_document_with_overla
p`, which asserts `chunks[i].text.split()[-32:] == chu
nks[i+1].text.split()[:32]` (at the smaller `chunk_tok
ens=6, overlap=2` scale that test uses). The
practical effect: a sentence or clause that happens to

(`test_spliokens`) becaus

the window would stall in place or walk backwards inst

**Document-boundary isolation.** Chunks are built per
`doc_id` and never merged across documents —
`by_doc: dict[str, list[Chunk]]` groups lines before a

where two different `doc_id`s' tokens end up in the sa

**Embedding model: `intfloat/multilingual-e5-base` (76

English-only alternative.** `configs/config.yaml` orig

`sentence-transformers/all-MiniLM-L6-v2` — a strong, f
